The book

LOCUTIONS

The Hidden Language of How You're Wired to Connect

What if you've spent your whole life trying to fix something that was never broken?

In Locutions, Rare Orion introduces a different way of recognizing the language of human connection: the patterns already present in how we reach for one another, express care, and seek belonging.

This isn't about learning to become someone better at connecting. It's about finally recognizing the connection that's been happening all along—including the parts of yourself you were taught to change.

About Rare

Rare Orion has spent their career exploring what it means to be human—from studying neuroscience and cardiopulmonary care to working in clinical laboratories, intensive care, and ocular and tissue recovery. Their curiosity eventually carried them beyond medicine and into science education, the performing arts, and digital storytelling.
